# Gonka Marketing Committee Report — August 2026

In August, the main work of the Marketing Committee focused on three areas:

- detailed review of contractor deliverables and preparation of structured feedback;
- ongoing follow-up on already funded marketing initiatives;
- review of new applications and proposals before potential funding.

The committee also involved active network participants in the review of selected materials and shared updates on ongoing work, so that participants voting on initiatives and people directly working with the product could see progress and emerging issues.

During August, the committee:

- completed detailed reviews of materials from **2 major TheSoul work stages — Offer 1.1 and Offer 1.2**;
- prepared **49 specific comments and recommendations**;
- of these, **30** were related to Positioning and **19** to the Brandbook;
- identified **at least 6 elements from the approved scope of work** that were missing or incomplete in the Brandbook;

During the Positioning review, comments from several network hosts were also taken into account.

## 1. Review of TheSoul Positioning

The committee carried out a detailed review of the Positioning document and prepared **30 specific comments** on individual slides and sections.

The review covered product logic, accuracy of Gonka mechanism descriptions, competitor analysis, target audience work, the basis for research conclusions, availability of sources, and the usefulness of the document for future work by other contractors.

The overall conclusion of the review was that the visual and communication parts of the document were developed in significantly more detail than the product and research parts. In several cases, it was not sufficiently clear how competitors were selected, how audience conclusions were made, or what evidence supported individual positioning elements.

### Examples of Issues Identified

**Incorrect description of network governance.**

The document stated that GNK is used to determine participation weight in governance. The committee pointed out that GNK is not used in voting: voting weight is based on the current computing power of hosts. The committee recommended removing this statement.

**Incorrect description of Sprint.**

The document suggested that Sprint verifies inference. The committee clarified that Sprint verifies the availability of computing power, while inference is checked through a separate statistical verification process.

**HeX was used as a source for understanding how users first discover Gonka.**

The committee noted that HeX was created specifically for Gonka and therefore cannot be used as independent evidence of how new users first discover the project.

**Reddit conclusions were made without real reach data.**

The document reviewed examples of Gonka mentions but did not include reach and engagement data for those publications. The committee requested information on sample size, sources, and data for at least one genuinely significant individual mention.

**The methodology for selecting competitors was not explained.**

For example, questions were raised about the inclusion of Filecoin. The document did not provide clear selection criteria or sources. The committee also suggested checking whether the main claims made by competitors match the real state of their products.

**Product-level comparison of competitors was missing.**

The committee requested a comparison of what competitors actually offer users, what their main advantages are based on, and where Gonka has objective differences.

**The host value proposition included an incorrect comparison.**

The document compared Gonka with the possibility for GPU operators to “rent out capacity to AWS,” although this model is not available to ordinary hosts.

**Some key use cases were not actually use cases.**

Several items described protocol mechanics or future development rather than specific use cases for developers and hosts.

In addition to these examples, the committee provided comments on audience segmentation, personas, measurability of positioning goals, the developer value proposition, and the separation of confirmed facts from research hypotheses.

## 2. Review of TheSoul Brandbook

The committee carried out a separate review of the Brandbook and prepared **19 specific comments and recommendations**.

In addition to reviewing the visual system, the document was compared with the scope of work approved by the community in the original proposal.

As a result, the committee identified **at least 6 approved elements that were missing or incomplete**.

### Elements from the Approved Scope of Work

**1. Logo clear space rules.**

This item was explicitly included in the proposal but was missing from the delivered Brandbook.

**2. Examples of incorrect logo usage.**

This section was also part of the original scope.

**3. Telegram templates.**

Telegram was explicitly listed among the platforms for which templates were expected.

**4. LinkedIn templates.**

LinkedIn was also included in the approved scope.

**5. Full photography guidelines.**

This section was included in the original proposal, but the Brandbook did not contain a complete set of rules for selecting, cropping, editing, and using photography.

**6. Font files.**

Delivery of the font files was also included in the original proposal. The committee additionally requested links to the relevant licenses for use on websites, social media, and printed materials.

### Practical Use of the Brandbook

The committee also requested:

- editable versions of templates, for example in Figma;
- a full package of working source files;
- vector versions of logos and key visual elements;
- source files for patterns, graphics, and icons;
- one accessible location for storing the final package of materials.

The committee also suggested clearer rules for grids, spacing, alignment, and pattern usage so that an external designer could work with the system independently.

The committee also raised the question of how the new visual identity should be used by brokers, media, and other independent Gonka participants, and suggested defining basic rules for how the new and original identities can coexist.

As additional recommendations outside the mandatory scope of work, the committee suggested developing a separate visual identity for the GNK token and rules for presenting key network metrics.

## 5. Review of the Omniverse Fest Proposal for 20,000 USDT

In August, the committee reviewed the Omniverse Fest proposal regarding possible Gonka participation in the event and sponsorship funding.

### Key Figures

- **20,000 USDT** — requested funding;
- **2 proposal versions** were reviewed — the original version and a lighter alternative;
- the organizers and their stated previous experience were verified;
- organizers, managers, and representatives of host teams were involved in the communication.

### What Was Done

The committee:

- verified the organizers and their connection to previously mentioned events, including in cases where some older websites and materials were no longer available;
- collected additional information through working communication with organizers, managers, and representatives of host teams;
- assessed the organizers’ previous experience, event readiness, relevance for Gonka, justification for sponsorship funding, and reputational context;
- noted that the requested **20,000 USDT** did not include all possible Gonka-related costs: additional expenses could be required for the team, booth, and supporting infrastructure;
- requested a **lighter commercial proposal** so that the community would have an alternative and less expensive participation option;
- received and reviewed the second version of the proposal;
- shared the main comments and recommendations with the organizers.

### Result

Following the review:

- the organizers received an assessment of the original **20,000 USDT** proposal, including the main questions and risks identified;
- an **alternative, less expensive participation option** was developed;
- after discussion and feedback, the organizers **decided not to move forward with the original format**;
- instead, a more limited Gonka presence was implemented through ecosystem representatives, a host team, and ambassadors;
- working relations with the organizers were maintained for possible future cooperation.

In August, the Marketing Committee worked at several stages of Gonka marketing initiatives: reviewing new proposals before potential funding, following up on already funded contractors, and conducting detailed reviews of completed deliverables.
