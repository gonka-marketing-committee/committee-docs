# Gonka Marketing Committee Charter

## About the Committee

Gonka Marketing Committee is a group of community contributors working with marketing initiatives across the Gonka ecosystem.

The committee has been operating in its current structure and format since August 1, 2026. This document describes the work of the current committee and does not apply to decisions or activities of previous committee structures.

The current committee was formed by community contributors who had already been reviewing marketing proposals and providing feedback on them.

Our role is to help the community better understand marketing initiatives and provide meaningful feedback on them.

The committee works in an advisory and contributor capacity. It supports the community on marketing-related initiatives, but it does not have separate governance authority, does not manage the network treasury, and does not replace the Foundation.

The name “Marketing Committee” does not mean that this is the only possible committee or team working on marketing.

Other marketing committees, teams, and working groups may appear in the Gonka ecosystem, including groups with similar or overlapping functions. The committee is open to working with them.

As the Foundation and new teams develop, the committee’s role and working format may also change.

## What the Committee Does

The Marketing Committee may contribute in the following areas:

- help with onboarding new marketing proposals, preparing feedback on them, and providing recommendation reports to miners and the community;
- participate in follow-up on funded initiatives and review their progress;
- assess whether results match approved proposals, deliverables, KPIs, and the current community roadmap;
- keep the community informed about progress, results, problems, and significant changes in marketing initiatives;
- prepare reports, assessments, and analytical materials related to marketing initiatives;
- identify gaps and tasks that are not currently covered in marketing work;
- suggest additions or updates to the community roadmap in marketing and related areas;
- organize AMA sessions and other open discussions when useful;
- propose initiatives, research, pilots, and tests of marketing hypotheses that may be useful for the development of the network.

The committee is not the only group that may perform these tasks. Other contributors, teams, and the Foundation may also participate in this work.

Committee recommendations and assessments are not binding for the network.

The committee decides its scope and working format based on current community needs, available funding, and the capacity of its contributors. This list describes possible areas of contribution, not a fixed or mandatory scope of work.

## Committee Membership

Committee members are contributors who:

- regularly participate in the committee’s work;
- have relevant experience or are ready to take responsibility for specific tasks;
- are ready to work together with other members of the group.

Any contributor may express interest in joining the committee.

The decision to add a new member is made by the current committee members, taking into account the contributor’s actual participation, relevant experience, and readiness to take responsibility for work.

One member may act as coordinator.

The coordinator organizes the work of the group but does not receive any additional governance authority within Gonka.

The coordinator is selected by the current committee members and may be replaced by their joint decision.

Advisors and mentors may contribute to specific tasks without becoming committee members.

### Ending Participation

Participation in the committee may end:

- voluntarily, at the member’s own decision;
- if a person does not participate in the committee’s work for an extended period or repeatedly does not complete responsibilities they agreed to take on;
- in case of serious misconduct that causes significant harm to Gonka, the committee, or trust in their work.

A decision to remove a member should not be based only on personal disagreements, criticism, or a different position on working matters.

## Reports

If the committee’s work is funded through an active proposal, the committee publishes reports for the relevant period.

During periods without active funding, reports may be published at the committee’s discretion.

Reports on funded work may include:

- key work and tasks completed by the committee;
- results and expected impact for the Gonka ecosystem;
- how the actual work compares with the goals, deliverables, and KPIs stated in the proposal;
- significant deviations, problems, and changes during the work;
- funding and main expenses;
- links to documents, research, reports, and other materials that can be published;
- when relevant, a list of additional materials that may be provided on request.

## Participation and Collaboration

The current committee structure was formed around the current tasks and workload.

As new tasks appear, the committee may invite new participants, especially contributors who are already active in the Gonka ecosystem and have relevant experience in marketing or related areas.

The committee is also open to working with individual contributors, teams, and experts without requiring them to formally join the committee.

Participation in the committee does not automatically mean a paid role.

A paid role or function is possible only when it is separately included in a proposal and receives community support.

For collaboration, contributors may contact any committee member.
