# Gonka Marketing Committee

Gonka Marketing Committee is a community contributor team working with marketing initiatives across the Gonka ecosystem.

The committee reviews marketing proposals and funded initiatives, provides feedback and analysis, follows their progress, and shares relevant findings with the community.

The committee works in an advisory and contributor capacity. It does not have separate governance authority, does not manage the network treasury, and does not replace the Foundation or other contributor teams.
